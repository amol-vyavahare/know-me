I've spent my career on the part of software where things break — first as a tester, then keeping
pipelines and platforms healthy, and more recently deciding *what* should be built in the first place.

This site is my working notebook: the projects I've shipped, the bugs that taught me the most, and
the things I learn along the way. The **persona switch** at the top re-arranges everything for the
role you're hiring for — QA, DevOps or Product Owner.

## How I work

- I start with the risk: what could go wrong, and how would we know?
- I write things down. Most of this site began as notes to my future self.
- I measure. If we can't see the effect of a change, we don't really know it worked.
