## Problem

38% of users who reached payment never completed it. The roadmap had a full checkout redesign planned.

## What we did

- Funnel analysis by device and payment method.
- 12 session-replay reviews and 6 customer calls.
- Found that one bank's OTP page timed out on mobile after 60 seconds.

## Decision

Paused the redesign. Shipped a retry flow and a partner escalation — two sprints instead of two quarters.
