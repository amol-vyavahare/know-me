## Testers think in risks

Every role I've had since QA has been about the same question: what could go wrong, and how would we know?

## DevOps is testing for the pipeline

Fast feedback, observability, safe rollbacks — it's the test pyramid applied to delivery.

## Product is testing ideas

Discovery is exploratory testing on a hypothesis before anyone writes code.
