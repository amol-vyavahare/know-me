## Start with the problem, not the button

"As a user I want a button" hides the why. I lead with the observed problem and the evidence.

## Acceptance examples, not acceptance essays

Three to five concrete Given/When/Then examples beat a page of prose. My QA years taught me that.

## Say what is out of scope

Half of refinement debates disappear when the story says what it will *not* do.
